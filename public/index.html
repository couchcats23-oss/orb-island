HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Everyone wears an IC chip in their left hand. Eliminate other players and pick up the chips they drop. Collect 3 chips, or be the last survivor, to win. When time runs out, the most chips (then health) wins.

The chip radar

Press Q to pulse your chip. It maps moving players within 150 m, but it also pings every chip in range, so they know a scan happened and where it came from. Two scans at nearly the same moment cancel each other out. Standing still keeps you off the radar.

Orbs

You carry up to eight on your bandolier. Walk over supply caches (glowing columns near landmarks) or fallen players' drops to restock. Hold left mouse to wind up a throw, release to throw. Right mouse is a short lob.
